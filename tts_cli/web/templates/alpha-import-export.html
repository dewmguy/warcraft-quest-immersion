Exact CSV contract

UTF-8, comma-delimited, one spoken quest stage or gossip line per row.

Download example
ColumnRequired content
sourceOne of accept, progress, complete, or gossip.
questNumeric quest ID; empty only for gossip.
quest_titleLocalized quest title; empty is allowed for gossip.
textLocalized dialogue text selected by the source query. It must not be empty.
DisplayRaceIDNumeric display race used to assign the baseline voice; -1 is the narrator/inanimate fallback.
DisplaySexID0 male, 1 female.
nameNPC, game object, or item delivering the line.
typeOne of creature, gameobject, or item.
idNumeric creature, game-object, or item entry ID.
original_textImmutable source text retained for review and addon identity.
Recommended joined fields

source_record_id or broadcast_text_id, zone_name, area_name, map_name, faction_name, role, occupation, rank, and subname. Any additional columns are retained as source metadata instead of discarded.
